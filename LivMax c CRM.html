✦ Questions we hear every day

Answered honestly.

We believe an educated client makes the best decisions. No jargon. No pressure. Just clear answers.

Do I need a medical exam to get covered? +
Most of our plans require no medical exam at all. We find the right carrier based on your health profile — quickly and without surprises. Many clients get approved same-day.
How much does life insurance actually cost? +
Less than most people think. A healthy 35-year-old can get $500,000 in coverage for around $25–$40/month. Your consultation is completely free — let's find your exact number with no obligation.
Is this only for people with families? +
Not at all. Single professionals use it for wealth building. Business owners use it for tax-free retirement and key person coverage. Life insurance is one of the most versatile financial tools available — for any life stage.
What if I already have life insurance through work? +
Group employer plans are usually 10x less coverage than what you actually need — and you lose them the moment you change jobs or get laid off. We'll show you the gap and fill it properly.
How long does it take to get covered? +
Some policies can be issued same-day. Most take 24–72 hours. We handle all the paperwork and communicate with the carrier on your behalf — you just approve and sign.
Can life insurance really replace a 401k for retirement? +
In many cases, it outperforms it. IUL (Indexed Universal Life) policies offer tax-free growth, tax-free withdrawals, zero market loss, and a death benefit — none of which a 401k provides simultaneously. We'll run the numbers for your specific situation.
